    Questions & answers

    How are questions answered?

    Each question is read alongside the asker's sign and birth details, when shared, and answered with a short written reading. Answers focus on themes, timing, and things to reflect on — not predictions set in stone.

    How long does an answer take?

    Questions are answered in the order they arrive, usually within a few days. Complex questions may take a little longer.

    Is my birth information kept private?

    In this standalone version, anything you enter stays in your own browser on your own device — it is not sent to a server. Only share what you're comfortable sharing.

    Can I ask about health, money, or legal matters?

    You can ask, but readings here are for entertainment and reflection only. They are not medical, financial, or legal advice. For decisions in those areas, please speak to a qualified professional.

    How is my zodiac sign decided?

    By your date of birth, using the tropical zodiac — the system most Western horoscopes use. If you were born near a sign changeover, your birth time and place can pin it down more precisely.

    About Ask the Stars

    Ask the Stars is a small corner of the internet for people who like to pause, look up, and wonder. It offers daily horoscopes, short zodiac readings, and a place to ask the questions that horoscopes alone don't answer.

    Astrology has been practiced for thousands of years as a way of telling stories about timing, character, and change. We keep that spirit: thoughtful, curious, and honest about what a reading can and cannot do.

    To get in touch, use the ask-a-question form above — it reaches the same place your questions do.
